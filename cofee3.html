Our Full Story

2023 - The Beginning

Started with ₦80k in a 2x2 kiosk at Unilag First Gate. Tired of watery Nescafe, we imported 5kg Ethiopia beans.

2024 - The Struggle

NEPA took light 3 days, beans burnt. We lost ₦30k. Moved to Yaba with generator. Students lined up 7am before lecture.

2025 - Yaba Roast House

Now roast every Monday & Thursday. 100% Arabica, single origin — Ethiopia, Rwanda, Colombia. No fake vanilla syrup.

2026 - 500 Cups Daily

Offices in Ikeja, Yaba, Surulere order daily. Promise: Never stale, never fake, always strong.
